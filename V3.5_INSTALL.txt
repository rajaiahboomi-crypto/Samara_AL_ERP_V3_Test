SAMARA CARE ERP V3.5 — PATIENT MASTER UPGRADE

1. Run in Supabase SQL Editor:
   supabase/13_V35_PATIENT_MASTER.sql

2. Upload the updated package contents to Samara_AL_ERP_V3_Test.

3. After deployment:
   - Open /VERSION and confirm 3.5.0
   - Press Ctrl + Shift + R
   - Open Patients → Edit

New patient fields:
- Date of Birth
- Age
- Gender
- Marital Status
- Blood Group
- Occupation
- Religion
- Preferred Language
- Nationality
- Aadhaar Number (Optional)
- Guardian Name
- Guardian Relationship

Formal prefixes:
- Male: Shri.
- Married/Widowed Female: Smt.
- Unmarried Female: Selvi.
